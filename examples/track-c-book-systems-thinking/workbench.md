## Part 3: Intuition Traps & Anti-Patterns

| Category | Intuition Trap | Real-World Failure Mode | Reliable Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| KPI Design ("Rule Beating") | "Measure lines of code / tickets closed to raise engineering productivity" | Engineers split trivial PRs and ignore high-leverage architectural refactors — **metric rises while true quality collapses** | Inspect what the metric ignores; align rewards with end-to-end system purpose, not easily gamed proxies |
| Scaling & Delays | "Hiring doubled last quarter and backlog hasn't dropped, so hire even faster" | Senior engineers lose capacity onboarding juniors (Brook's delay loop), causing a massive over-hiring bust 9 months later | Account for **onboarding/pipeline delays** and measure net capacity *after* training drag before adjusting inflows |
