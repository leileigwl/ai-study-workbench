## Part 3: Common Misconception Traps

| Area | Literal Translation Trap | How It Sounds to Native Speakers | Grounded Natural Expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refusal | "No, I can't finish by Friday." | Blunt, defensive, ends collaboration | "Friday is going to be tight if we include X—what if we ship Y first?" |
| Apology | "Sorry, I am too slow." | Makes you look unreliable even when scope doubled | "I want to make sure we don't rush testing before the demo." |
