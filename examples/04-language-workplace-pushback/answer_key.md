### Q1.1 Why does "No, impossible, I have too much work" hurt, and what 3 elements fix it?
- **Standard Answer Key Points (`3`)**:
  1. **Acknowledge the Shared Goal**: Show alignment before saying no (*"I see why you want that for Friday..."*).
  2. **Frame Around Quality/Scope (Not Personal Overload)**: Point to testing, accuracy, or competing priorities.
  3. **Offer a Concrete Plan B**: Propose two clear options (*"Core flow Friday, export Tuesday"* or *"Full bundle Tuesday"*).
- **Follow-Up Probes if Missed (Never spoil directly)**:
  - *If missing Point 1*: `"Your manager reads 'impossible' and thinks you're brushing off the launch. Write 5 opening words showing you're on the same team."`

### Q1.2 Spoken ways to suggest cutting/splitting scope naturally
- **Standard Answer Key Points (`2`)**:
  1. **Priority Question**: *"If Friday is a hard cutoff, which piece matters most for leadership to see?"*
  2. **Phase-Split Verbs**: *prioritize*, *split into phase two*, *tight*.
- **Follow-Up Probes if Missed**:
  - *If stiff/textbook*: `"That sounds like a written contract. Say the same thing as if chatting on Slack with a teammate."`
