## Part 3: Common Mistakes (Quick Trap Check)

| Area | Literal / Textbook Mistake | Why It Sounds Off to Native Speakers | Natural Spoken Alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refusal | "No, I can't finish by Friday." | Sounds blunt, defensive, and shuts down collaboration | "Friday is going to be tight if we include X—can we ship Y first?" |
| Over-apologizing | "Sorry, sorry, my fault, I am too slow." | Makes you look incompetent even when the scope is unreasonable | "I want to make sure we don't cut corners on testing." |
| Rigid phrasing | "Please wait until next Tuesday." | Sounds like issuing a command rather than proposing options | "How would Tuesday morning work for you?" |
