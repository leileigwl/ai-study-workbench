## Part 3: Common Misconception Traps

| Area | Tempting Student Intuition | How It Fails in Practice | Grounded Truth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price Caps | "A \$500 rent cap lets families afford the exact same apartments" | Demand surges while builders build offices instead -> fewer units exist | A **binding price ceiling** creates persistent queues, lower maintenance, and under-the-table fees |
| Curve Shifts | "Higher prices reduce demand!" | Confuses *moving along a fixed demand curve* with shifting preferences | Price changes move **along** a curve; external shocks (income, buzz, input costs) **shift** the curve |
