## Part 3: Common Mistakes (Quick Trap Check)

| Area | Tempting Student Intuition | What Actually Happens | Reliable Economic Principle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price Caps | "Capping price at \$500 lets poor families rent the same apartments cheaper" | Demand surges at \$500 while landlords convert rentals to condos or skip repairs -> fewer units available | A **binding price ceiling** keeps $Q_d > Q_s$, creating queues, lower quality, and under-the-table fees |
| Curve Shifts | "Higher prices mean lower demand!" | You confused a *demand curve shift* with *moving along the demand curve* after supply shrinks | Price changes move **along** a curve; outside shocks (income, trends, costs) **shift** the curve |
